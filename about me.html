Biography

I am Anastasija Koceva, third year student in Dsu Rtsoo "Kole Nehtenin". I come from Zletovo, an old village in Probistip. Born on 14 of January 2008.
From my earliest years, art has been my passion. I spent years polishing my skills as a hobby and now do commissions as a freelance artist. I am also open to learning and developing different skills, like web design, game development, writing... I am ambitious and willing to take on challenges that help me learn something new and face my shortcomings.
My goal is to create something that is my own and inspire people to do the same.

CV

Activities
The Festival of Fine arts

I participated in the Festival of Fine Arts at the University "Goce Delchev" - held on 15.5.2024 and 16.5.2024. We created the works in acrylic technique and pencil with various motifs, from still life, landscape, portrait, to figurative and abstract compositions.

First Lego League

I participated in the First Lego League for three years, as a member and mentor of the RoboRox team, winning two awards, Team for Engineering Excellence and third place in the Core Values ​​category.

Financial Literacy for Young Entrepreneurs

Within the framework of the project "Financial Literacy for Young Entrepreneurs" implemented by Cefe Macedonia, financially supported by the US Embassy in the Republic of Macedonia, I was a member of a training for students on the topic "Micro Business Game".

Hackatron of Ideas

"Ideas Hackathon" was held at the Youth Center Probishtip. At the event, we developed a solution to a community problem and competed in designing ideas. During the event, we worked together with a mentor, with the aim of implementing the supported idea.
