Terms of service


PAYMENTS
The first half of the payment is given before starting the commission, and the other half after the first update of the progress.

REVISIONS
Unlimited revisions are available in the first update. After the commission is finished, two revisions are free, any more extra revisions will be charged additionally.

DEADLINES AND DELIVERY
The deadline is one month, but the delivery can be expected within the first week.

USE
Commissions are only for personal use. No feeding art in AI. I'm allowed to post the work online while clearly stating that it's a commission. If the client is not okay with this, please inform me beforehand.

REFUNDS
Refunds are available only if I'm unable to finish a commission by the given deadline and if the refund is requested before any updates on the progress.

COMMUNICATION
Regular updates and expected feedback from the client before continuing with the commission.
